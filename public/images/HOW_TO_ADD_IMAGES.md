# How to Add Images to Homepage

## Step 1: Prepare Your Images
- Format: JPG, PNG, or WebP
- Recommended size: 1920x1080 pixels (or similar wide format)
- File size: Optimize for web (under 500KB each recommended)

## Step 2: Name Your Images
Place your images in this folder (`public/images/`) with these exact names:

- `hero-1.jpg` - First hero slide image
- `hero-2.jpg` - Second hero slide image  
- `hero-3.jpg` - Third hero slide image
- `hero-4.jpg` - Fourth hero slide image
- `hero-5.jpg` - Fifth hero slide image
- `hero-6.jpg` - Sixth hero slide image
- `hero-7.jpg` - Seventh hero slide image

## Step 3: Add Your Images
1. Copy your image files
2. Paste them into: `public/images/` folder
3. Rename them to match the names above (hero-1.jpg, hero-2.jpg, etc.)

## Step 4: Refresh Your Browser
After adding images, refresh your browser at `http://localhost:3000` and the images will automatically appear!

## Notes:
- You can use fewer than 7 images - just name them hero-1.jpg, hero-2.jpg, etc.
- The component will automatically use your local images if they exist
- If images don't exist, it will use fallback images from Unsplash
- Supported formats: .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp
