# Videos Directory

## Video Files Needed

### 1. Promotional Video (Modal)
**File Name**: `essaouira-promo.mp4`
- **Location**: `public/videos/essaouira-promo.mp4`
- **Usage**: Plays in modal when clicking "Watch Video" button
- **Format**: MP4
- **Recommended**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1280x720 (HD)
- **Duration**: 1-3 minutes
- **File Size**: Under 50MB recommended

### 2. Background Video (Hero Section)
**File Name**: `essaouira-background.mp4`
- **Location**: `public/videos/essaouira-background.mp4`
- **Usage**: Background video in section after Hero
- **Format**: MP4
- **Recommended**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Duration**: 30 seconds - 2 minutes (will loop)
- **File Size**: Under 30MB recommended (optimize for web)
- **Important**: Should be muted and loop seamlessly

## Video Requirements

### General Requirements
- **Format**: MP4 (recommended) or WebM
- **Codec**: H.264 for best browser compatibility
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD) or 1280x720 (HD)
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 (widescreen)

### Optimization Tips
- Compress videos to reduce file size
- Use H.264 codec for compatibility
- Keep background video short (30-60 seconds) as it loops
- Ensure smooth looping (last frame should match first frame)

## How to Add Videos

1. Prepare your video files
2. Name them exactly as specified above
3. Place them in: `public/videos/`
4. Refresh your browser

## Supported Formats

- `.mp4` (recommended - best browser support)
- `.webm` (good compression, modern browsers)
- `.ogg` (fallback option)
